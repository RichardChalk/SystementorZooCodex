Animal friends

Say hello to some zoo favorites

Meet six friendly animals that curious kids love to spot around the park.

Lion

A sunny-maned big cat that loves resting, roaring, and watching the world.

Elephant

A gentle giant with clever ears, a strong trunk, and a calm walk.

Giraffe

A tall leaf-snacker with long legs and a curious, friendly face.

Zebra

A striped runner whose bold coat makes every group look different.

Penguin

A waddling swimmer that looks dressed up and loves chilly water.

Turtle

A slow shell-walker that reminds everyone to explore at their own pace.
